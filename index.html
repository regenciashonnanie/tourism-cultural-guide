    Traditions that bring us together

    Culture is part of the journey.

    The island’s heritage is alive in its Holy Week streets, its welcome rituals, and the stories people pass along.

    HOLY WEEK · MARINDUQUE

    A Lenten tradition

    The Moriones Festival

    During Holy Week, costumed Moriones wearing vivid Roman-centurion masks parade through communities in a dramatic retelling of the story of Longinus. The tradition is especially associated with Boac, Mogpog, and Gasan. Dates and activities vary by town each year; ask local organizers for the current program.

    Observe respectfully · Ask before photographing people
    01

    Boac’s hilltop church

    Visit the Immaculate Conception Cathedral and wander Boac’s old-town streets with care for active places of worship and community life.

    BOAC · HISTORIC LANDMARK
    02

    Welcome with a putong

    Putong (also called putungan) is a Marinduque welcome ritual of song, a flower crown, and hospitality. It is shared on special occasions, not a performance on demand.

    ISLAND-WIDE · LIVING TRADITION
    03

    Remember Pulang Lupa

    The Battle of Pulang Lupa memorial in Torrijos honors a significant Filipino victory in the Philippine–American War and invites a moment of reflection.
